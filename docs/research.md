## Annual bill and scoring

AUD 37,500 annualised contract value: the Tasmanian Department of Justice published an AUD 75,000 Siteimprove Web Governance Tool contract covering February 2023 to February 2025, excluding GST. Dividing the two-year value by two gives the annualised selection figure. This is a historical public-sector contract, not a current small-business invoice or proof of cash paid. [Primary disclosure, page 76](https://www.justice.tas.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0010/728569/Department-of-Justice-Annual-Report-2022-23-web-compressed.pdf).

Separately, the UK public-sector list records GBP 13,000 as a recurring licence for 5,000 pages. Its billing period is not stated in that price sheet, so it is not described here as an annual rate. [Vendor-authored list filed on the UK Digital Marketplace](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/documents/702601/973395868511278-pricing-document-2024-05-07-1303.pdf).

The vendor's pricing route redirects to its homepage. No numeric price is therefore used on our comparison page. The registry prices list is empty. [Pricing route checked this run](https://www.siteimprove.com/pricing/).

## Evidence behind product and page claims

- [Siteimprove Cross Site Accessibility Issues](https://help.siteimprove.com/support/solutions/articles/80001064337-widget-cross-site-accessibility-issues): cross-site filtering, page drill-down and CSV downloads. Column layouts can vary; our fixture is synthetic.
- [Siteimprove fixed issues](https://help.siteimprove.com/support/solutions/articles/80000448131-where-to-view-fixed-accessibility-issues): fixed-issue reporting exists, but the module does not identify who corrected an issue. This is the narrowly supported differentiation. We do not claim its dashboards cannot answer all ten questions.
- [Siteimprove accessibility](https://www.siteimprove.com/platform/accessibility/): testing and accessibility management scope. Our own database does not reproduce its scanner.
- [Siteimprove success plans](https://www.siteimprove.com/success-plans/): support and advisory packages exist. We do not assign invented prices to them.
- [Capterra pricing and review](https://www.capterra.com/p/164806/Siteimprove/pricing/): one review reports cancellation to save money. This is qualitative evidence, not a savings calculation.
- [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), [W3C evaluation guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/), [NZ Web Accessibility Standard 1.2](https://www.digital.govt.nz/standards-and-guidance/nz-government-web-standards/web-accessibility-standard-1-2) and [NZ privacy principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) inform the scoped record checks in compliance.md.

No real customer data, quotes, invoices or secret values were used. Every demo command capture comes from running this build on synthetic data.
