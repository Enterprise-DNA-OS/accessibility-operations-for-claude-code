# Record checks and sources

Checked 28 October 2026. These checks review evidence and record completeness. They do not evaluate a website or certify compliance. No regulated compliance service is supplied.

| Rule in compliance output | What is checked | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CRITERION | A finding has a criterion reference | WCAG criteria identify the requirement being assessed. Imported references remain operator-supplied text. |
| EVIDENCE | A recorded fix has a current passing retest | Local review rule. A pass is tied to the finding, page and fix revision. |
| MANUAL-SCOPE | A completed manual or combined audit is recorded for a site | Local scope reminder informed by W3C evaluation guidance. A recorded audit alone proves no coverage or conformance. |
| OWNERSHIP | An unresolved finding has an owner and due date | Local operating rule, not a statutory deadline. |
| EXCEPTION | A risk decision is unexpired and matches the current record | Local risk review rule. No legal exemption is implied. |
| REVIEW | A site has a current review date | Local operating rule, not a mandated frequency. |
| AUDIT | A planned audit has not passed its agreed deadline | Local operating rule. |
| RETENTION | A site marked as holding personal information has a current purpose-review date | NZ Privacy Act principle 9. The chosen review date is local policy, not a statutory retention period. |

## Accessibility references

[WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) supplies success criteria and conformance requirements. Demo references include 1.1.1 Non-text Content, 2.1.1 Keyboard, 2.4.7 Focus Visible, 3.3.2 Labels or Instructions and 4.1.3 Status Messages. These are criterion labels, not an assessment of your website. Store the actual assessment scope, method, evaluator and evidence. Automated results cannot establish all conformance requirements.

[W3C evaluation guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/) explains evaluation tools and human evaluation. The system records supplied test results and their method. It never executes a test or verifies that an evidence reference exists.

[NZ Government Web Accessibility Standard 1.2](https://www.digital.govt.nz/standards-and-guidance/nz-government-web-standards/web-accessibility-standard-1-2) applies to specified government organisations from 17 March 2025 and requires WCAG 2.2 Level AA subject to its stated exceptions. Do not apply this mandate automatically to every private business. The demo jurisdiction label asks for a scope review. It does not claim the fictional organisation is legally in scope. Australian teams must set their applicable requirements with their own adviser.

[Privacy principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) concerns retaining personal information only while a lawful purpose exists. A review flag prompts a human decision. Nothing is deleted automatically.

## Evidence and access

Every mutation records the supplied actor. Actors are not authenticated identities. Evidence fields are references, not uploaded or independently validated documents. Database administrators can alter history. Shared operation needs access controls, backups, retention rules and protected original evidence. Reports distinguish open findings, internal risk acceptance and recorded resolution. No score or certificate is generated.
